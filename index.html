About the Guild

The Seattle Printers Guild is a group of artists that organize fun, free printmaking events available to all. We also operate Paper Pushers, a consignment zine shop and art space at 1200 Fifth Ave in downtown Seattle. Stop by to check out zines, artist books, and indie publications of all genres, or drop in to one of our free workshops about print and art-making, urban sketching, bookbinding, and more!
